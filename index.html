✨ TinkerHub Campus Chapter

Not a club. A movement

TinkerHub is a community-led tech movement built by students, for students — peer-to-peer, hands-on, and driven entirely by volunteers who'd rather build something real than sit through another slide deck.

This is TinkerHub's chapter at JCET — student-run, community-driven, with no gatekeeping and no hierarchy. Just people who'd rather build than watch, and an open invitation to anyone curious enough to show up.

💡

Learn by building

Workshops and challenges over lectures — every session ends with something you made.

🌱

Student-run, always

Planned and run entirely by students — no gatekeeping, just people who show up.

🤝

Open to everyone

No prior coding experience needed — just curiosity and a willingness to tinker.
